We think like a marketing team and shoot like a film crew.

We're Volare Media, a video production and marketing company based in Victoria, working across Vancouver Island, British Columbia and Canada. Marketing videos that perform are the core of what we do, and we bring the same film craft to documentaries, music videos and film.

Home base is Victoria, and we film across the whole Island, from Sooke and the West Shore up through the Cowichan Valley, Nanaimo, Parksville and the Comox Valley to Campbell River, plus Vancouver, the Okanagan and anywhere else in British Columbia.

Film craft meets marketing strategy.

Industry experience

Our roots are in film production: cinematography, sound, directing and crew discipline that carries into every commercial project.

Performance thinking

We build videos around a goal: broadcast reach, ad conversion, social engagement. Authentic content that performs in digital spaces.

Videos made with intention

Every video starts with the goal, the audience and where it will run, before anyone picks up a camera. We never make a video just to make a video.

A well-run set makes a better film.

Volare Media is a crew-first production company. That means clear roles, fair treatment and a thoughtful crew structure on every shoot. It's the right way to work, and it's also why our footage looks the way it does. People do their best work when the set runs well.
