On set

A small crew,
on your turf.

Nobody on your staff signed up to be on camera, so we don't treat them like talent on a deadline. We keep the crew small, the mood easy, and we interview instead of scripting. Offices, shops, warehouses, job sites: we scout ahead, bring only the gear the job needs and schedule around your busiest hours. One planned day routinely produces a website film, interview material, a training module and b-roll your team reuses for months.

How it works

A day of ours
for an hour of yours.

(001) Plan

We do the homework

A call to pin down the goal and where the video will live, a shot list, and pre-interview calls so nobody meets us for the first time on camera.

(002) Shoot

One day, on site

A compact crew at your office, shop or job site, working around your operations. Your people walk in, sit down, film, and get back to their day.

(003) Deliver

Every cut you need

The website film, the internal versions, LinkedIn cutdowns, captions, and files sized for your intranet, your inbox or your sales deck. Same colour, sound and finish as our broadcast work.

Common questions

Asked, answered.

Location

Can you film at our office?

Almost always yes, and it's usually better. Your space tells the story. We scout ahead, keep the footprint small and work around your day.

Nerves

Our people aren't actors.

Good. We interview, we don't script. A pre-call, a calm set and the right questions get the take where someone says the true thing, plainly.

Value

Can we batch several videos?

That's how we prefer to work. Getting a crew in the building is the expensive part, so once we're there we capture everything the plan calls for.

Support

Scripts and teleprompter?

Both, when the piece needs them. Executive messages and training often do. Interviews and culture pieces almost never should.
