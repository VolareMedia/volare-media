The crew

Meet the
department heads.

Every Volare Media project runs like a film set: clear departments, clear roles, and one person who owns each craft. These are the people who pick up when you call, and the crew behind the work you see on this site.

The call sheet

Heads of
department.

( 001 ) · Marketing

Chris Chisholm

Founder · Producer · Editor · DP

Chris built Volare Media where film craft meets marketing strategy. With a background in creative direction and post-production, he brings a results-driven, cinematic eye to marketing videos, commercials and branded content. Credits include producing and editing the Tailgate Toolkit TV commercial, and shooting Senior Discount, the documentary Pagliacci's and the multi-award-winning Red Eye as director of photography.

chris@volaremedia.net

( 002 ) · Film

Jackie Taylor

Producer · Director · Writer · Props Master

Jackie leads the film department with a multi-faceted background in producing, directing, writing and art department work, and experience across indie, commercial and large-scale film and series productions. Her creative vision and production expertise have shaped dozens of stories from concept to completion.

jackie@volaremedia.net

Beyond the heads

It takes a bigger
crew than this.

Department heads are the constant. Around them, we build the right crew for each project from a trusted bench of Vancouver Island film professionals: gaffers, grips, sound recordists, hair and makeup, production assistants. Crew-first isn't just how we treat our own team. It's how we treat everyone on the call sheet.
