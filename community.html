Causes we're proud to support.

Some of the work that means the most to us never sold a single product. Volare Media loves partnering with nonprofits, charities and fundraisers, telling the stories that raise money, raise awareness and genuinely fill our hearts.

Why we film for charities.

Video has a way of reaching people that a microphone and a podium can't. Visuals and sound together get past the facts and into the emotion, and for a good cause, that emotion is the whole point. That's why we bring the exact same film craft to a fundraiser that we bring to any commercial client. Same crew, same standard, same care. It's some of the most rewarding work we get to do.

The Tailgate Toolkit.

Read the story

Not every cause we care about is a gala. The Tailgate Toolkit is a mental-health initiative for BC's construction community, an industry that loses far too many of its own to a crisis that too often goes unspoken. We made the campaign that carried its message onto 61 radio and TV stations across the province. It's some of the most important work we've ever pointed a camera at.

Peninsula Gives.

Peninsula Gives is a local charity that throws unforgettable events to raise money for BC Children's Hospital. They were the ones who first brought us into this world, and every year since, we've been proud to help their story reach further than any room ever could.

On every one of these, Volare Media was the media sponsor. We donated all of our time and resources to make these films happen, because we believe in what Peninsula Gives does.

Shine Gala.

The Shine Gala is a dazzling Victoria evening of fashion, food and fundraising, all in support of BC Children's Hospital. In 2024 we were honoured to join in and help tell the stories at its heart.

Volare Media came on as media sponsor, donating all of our time and resources to make these films, because the families behind them deserve to be seen.

Fashion Splash.

Fashion Splash is a Victoria fashion showcase with a purpose: every year it raises money for the Victoria Women's Transition House, the organization that gives women and children a safe place to go when they're leaving violence and abuse.

For the 2024 event we came on as a media sponsor, donating our time and resources to produce a film that put the cause front and centre.

Volare Media · Media Sponsor · 2024

2024. Media Sponsor · Fundraiser film

The 2024 Fashion Splash gathered Vancouver Island's designers, models and beauty professionals for a night in support of the Victoria Women's Transition House, marking the organization's 50th year. We produced the film that carried its message to the room.
