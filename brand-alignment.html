Why it matters

Your video buys the attention.
Your brand keeps it.

We kept seeing great marketing videos do their job, then lose people the moment they landed on a website or Instagram page that felt like a different company. When the ad, the landing page, your socials and your Google profile all look and sound the same, every touchpoint builds trust instead of breaking it, and every dollar you spend on marketing goes further.

Common questions

Asked, answered.

Timing

How long does brand alignment take?

It depends on how many places your brand lives and whether a website rebuild is part of it. After the first audit we give you a clear timeline and price, and we roll everything out together at the end so nothing is left half-changed.

Logo

Do we have to change our logo?

No. If your logo works, we keep it and clean it up into a proper set of files: a main logo, a compact badge and a horizontal version. If it's holding you back, we can redesign it or take you through a full rebrand.

Website

Can you rebuild our website as part of it?

Yes. The website is usually where a drifted brand shows most, and it's where your ads and videos send people. We refresh or rebuild it as part of the package, or work with your existing site if it's in good shape.

New brands

We're a brand new business. Is this for us?

It's the best time to do it. Building the name, logo, colours, message and brand guide together, then launching everywhere at once, means you never drift in the first place.
