It's A Doodle K9 Service.

A much-loved Labradoodle breeder in Sooke, a brand video that was already working, and a brand that had grown in layers. On the left, a section of their current homepage. On the right, the top of the new design.

Three logo versions in use today. All of them are being replaced.

Two names in use: It's A Doodle Labradoodles on the logos, It's A Doodle K9 Service in the website's top bar.

One name. One brand. Everywhere.

  • The name. Back to the original: It's A Doodle K9 Service, used the same way everywhere.
  • All-new logos and design. A fresh logo set and visual style, developed with the owners and approved together.
  • A full brand guide. Logos, colours, type, voice and templates, so it stays aligned for years.
  • A rebuilt website, centred on the brand video we made for them.
  • Instagram and Facebook, rebuilt and realigned: profile images, covers, bios and highlights.
  • Google, upgraded and connected. Their Google Business Profile refreshed and linked to every social platform.
After

The finished brand and website launch in 2027, with the new name and logos in place. We'll update this page when they go live.
