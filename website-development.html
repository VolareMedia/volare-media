How it works

From first call
to launch day.

A clear process, and the same people from the first call to long after the site goes live.

  1. (01)

    Discovery call

    We look at your current website if you have one, your goals and who you need to reach, then give you a clear plan and price.

  2. (02)

    Plan & design

    Sitemap, page designs and copy written for the searches your customers actually make, built around your brand and your video.

  3. (03)

    Build

    Fast, mobile-first, search-ready pages, with an online store, booking or lead forms wherever you need them.

  4. (04)

    Launch

    Redirects from your old pages, Google Search Console, analytics and ad tracking set up, so nothing gets lost in the switch.

  5. (05)

    Manage & grow

    Updates, SEO, blog posts, ads and fresh video from the same team, so the site keeps working long after launch.

Common questions

Asked, answered.

Scope

New build or rebuild?

Both. Blank page or aging site, we take it from there.

We design, write and build new websites from scratch, and we rebuild any existing website, keeping what works, replacing what doesn't, and moving you onto a fast, modern, search-ready foundation.

Starting point

Just a website?

Yes. Plenty of clients start there on its own.

The full package usually starts with a new build or rebuild of your website. Once it's live, brand alignment, video and marketing campaigns can layer on whenever you're ready.

Brand

What about our existing brand?

Solid? We align to it. Scattered? We fix it. New? We build it.

If your brand is working, everything we make follows it. If it's inconsistent, our brand alignment service audits every touchpoint and brings them into one look and one voice. Starting fresh? We build the brand from the ground up.

Team

Who does the work?

Our own support team of media and tech specialists.

The filmmakers, editors, designers and developers behind Volare Media, supported by trusted production partners when a project needs extra hands. People who already know your story.

E-commerce

Do you build online stores?

Yes. Shopify stores, set up to sell and to grow.

We design and build Shopify stores with product pages, collections, wholesale and checkout, then help them grow with SEO, content and ads. De Mode en Vogue's store is one we revamped and now manage.

SEO

Will a rebuild hurt our rankings?

Not when it's planned properly.

Before launch we map every old page to its new address with redirects, keep the content that already ranks, and submit the new site to Google Search Console, so the search traffic you've earned follows you to the new site.
