De Mode en Vogue

Her marketing, website, videos and online store. Nov 16, 2026

We’re proud to be the marketing partner for De Mode en Vogue. Danica sells beautiful tights made in Italy, and we help more people find them.

We designed her website and run all of her e-commerce, from her Shopify store to her Amazon listings. We also produce her videos, including her commercial, “When Your Outfit Is Missing Something,” and run her campaigns on Google and Meta.

What we do together

  • Marketing
  • Website design
  • Video production
  • Google and Meta campaigns
  • Shopify and Amazon

SVI Automotive

Their website, videos, social media and ads. Nov 9, 2026

We’re proud to be the marketing partner for SVI Automotive. They supply, install and service lifts and shop equipment across Vancouver Island.

We built their website and keep it growing with a monthly blog. We make three social posts a week, run their Google and Meta ads, and make their videos, from customer testimonials to vertical video ads.

What we do together

  • Website and monthly blog
  • Customer testimonial videos
  • Vertical video ads
  • Social media, three posts a week
  • Google and Meta ads
