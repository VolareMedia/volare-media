The documentaries we make.

Documentary filmmaking tells a real story with real people. We film it for the screen, for film productions and for brands.

  • Documentaries Independent, fully funded and network documentaries, built on real interviews and story-driven questions.
  • EPK and behind the scenes The electronic press kit a film needs for its publicity: cast and crew interviews, B-roll and behind-the-scenes footage.
  • Branded documentaries Brand stories told as documentary-style films: the real story behind a company or a product.

Why make one? People back brands that feel real: 88% of consumers say authenticity matters when they choose which brands to support (Nosto, 2021). For a film, the EPK has to be shot during production. Distributors, festivals and funders ask for it, and you can't go back for it later.
